Workspace Todos & Real-Time Collaboration

Every account automatically receives a personal workspace with starter tasks on first signup via PostgreSQL triggers. Switch workspaces anytime from the header selector.

1. Creating, Filtering & Prioritizing Tasks
  • Statuses: Track items across todo, in_progress, and done.
  • Priorities: Categorize tasks as low, medium, high, or urgent.
  • Nullable PATCH Fields: Clearing description, due date, or assignee sends an explicit null using three-state deserialize_double_option semantics.
2. Real-Time SSE & Optional R2 Image Attachments

Connected tabs subscribe to /api/notifications/sse so task mutations and notifications refresh across collaborators automatically.

When APP__R2__* credentials are configured, attached images are WebP-compressed in the browser, XOR-obfuscated before direct presigned upload, and decrypted on demand by ProtectedImage.svelte.
