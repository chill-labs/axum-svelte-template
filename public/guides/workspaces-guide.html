Multi-Tenant Workspaces & Granular RBAC

Isolate tasks, attachments, and collaborator access per workspace. Your active selection is persisted on users.active_workspace_id and cached in Moka.

1. Role Presets vs. Custom Permission Matrix
  • Owner: Implicitly bypasses granular checks and holds full workspace authority.
  • Editor / Member / Viewer: Pre-populated subsets of the 12 Resource × Action grants (Workspace, Members, Todos × View / Create / Edit / Delete).
  • Custom: Fine-tune individual JSONB permission flags per member in /workspaces/members.
Tip: Deleting a workspace is guarded against removing your last remaining workspace (CANNOT_DELETE_LAST_WORKSPACE).
